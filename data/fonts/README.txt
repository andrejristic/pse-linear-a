Drop "NotoSansLinearA-Regular.ttf" here to render real Linear A glyphs
(Unicode block U+10600..U+1077F). Without it the app falls back to GORILA
sign numbers (AB01, ...). The font is not bundled to keep the repo license-clean;
fetch it from the Noto project and place it in this folder (it is then packaged
into the .deb under /opt/pse-linear-a/data/fonts).
